1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use FreeUpper, we may collect the following categories of information.

1.1 Account and Profile Information

When you register or sign in, we may process information such as:

  • Your name and email address.
  • Your profile picture.
  • Your username and FreeUpper user ID.
  • Your biography and other profile information you choose to provide.
  • Authentication identifiers associated with Google sign-in.

Google provides information according to the permissions you authorize during sign-in. We do not receive your Google password through Google sign-in.

1.2 Content You Create

We may collect and store content you submit or upload, including:

  • Text posts, comments, and replies.
  • Photos, videos, and audio.
  • Profile images and other uploaded media.
  • Marketplace listings and related product information.
  • Messages and attachments sent through communication features.
  • Other content you choose to share through FreeUpper.

Content visibility depends on the feature, your settings, and the functionality available in the application.

1.3 Communications and Support

If you contact us, report content, submit feedback, or request account assistance, we may process the information you provide and records needed to respond.

1.4 Technical and Usage Information

Where collected, we may process technical information such as browser or device information, IP address, diagnostic information, security events, and basic usage data. The information collected depends on the features and services enabled in FreeUpper and its infrastructure.

We do not claim to collect every category listed above in every session.

2. How We Use Information

We use information for purposes including:

  • Creating and managing user accounts.
  • Authenticating users and maintaining account sessions.
  • Creating and displaying profiles.
  • Hosting and delivering posts, photos, videos, audio, and other media.
  • Supporting feeds, comments, messaging, and marketplace features.
  • Maintaining the security and reliability of FreeUpper.
  • Detecting spam, fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access.
  • Responding to support requests and account deletion requests.
  • Troubleshooting errors and improving the service.
  • Meeting applicable legal obligations and enforcing our policies.

We do not use information for purposes that are incompatible with applicable law without an appropriate legal basis.

3. Google Sign-In

FreeUpper supports signing in with Google.

When you choose Google sign-in, Google processes information according to its own privacy policy and the permissions presented during authentication. FreeUpper may receive basic profile information, such as your name, email address, profile picture, and account identifier, as permitted by the sign-in configuration.

We use this information to authenticate you and associate your account with your FreeUpper profile.

Google’s privacy policy is available at: https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can manage your Google account’s third-party connections through your Google Account settings.

4. How Your Content Is Shared

FreeUpper includes social and community features.

  • Public content: Posts, profile information, comments, videos, and marketplace listings may be visible to other users or the public, depending on the feature and your settings.
  • Messages: Messages are intended for their recipients, but you should not assume they are end-to-end encrypted unless FreeUpper explicitly confirms that protection.
  • Marketplace information: Listings and information you choose to display may be visible to prospective buyers and other users.
  • Content you share: Other users may copy, screenshot, download, or redistribute content that they can access.

Please avoid publishing passwords, financial account details, private identification documents, or other sensitive information in public posts or messages.

Deleting content from FreeUpper may not remove copies already downloaded, shared, cached, or retained by other users or third-party systems.
